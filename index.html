Biography

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Signal Theory, Telematics and Communications, University of Granada, Spain. My current research focuses on clinical applications of speech technology, brain-computer interfaces and speech biometrics.

Projects

Clinical Applications of NeuroScience

NeuroLengua aims to investigate the neural substrates of language with the goal enhancing the lives of patients affected by epilepsy and paralysis.

Voice Restoration with Silent Speech Interfaces

ReSSInt aims to investigate the use of silent speech interfaces to restore communication in people who have been deprived of the ability to speak.

Secure Voice Biometrics Systems Using Deep Processing Techniques

BioVoz explores security challenges in voice biometrics using deep neural networks for fraud detection.
